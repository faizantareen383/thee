FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Browse the collection, add your favorite pieces to the cart, and complete checkout with the JazzCash demo payment flow.

What payment method do you accept?

For this frontend-only version, the website displays JazzCash as the only payment method.

Do you accept custom orders?

Yes, we create custom resin designs for gifts, home decor, and brand gifting. Use the custom order form to request a design.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery across Pakistan typically takes 2 to 5 business days, depending on the size and customization requirements.

Can I return a product?

Returns are accepted for damaged or incorrect orders. Contact us and our team will guide you through the process.

How should resin products be cleaned?

Use a soft, dry cloth and avoid harsh chemicals, hot surfaces, or abrasive cleaners to maintain the finish.

How can I track my order?

After checkout, you can visit the account or orders page to view the tracking timeline and current status.

Is JazzCash available?

Yes, this demo storefront presents JazzCash as the payment option for this static version.
